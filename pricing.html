Pricing questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — request an upgrade or downgrade from your Subscription page anytime. A Developer applies it, and your next invoice reflects the new plan's price.

What happens if a payment is late?

There's a short grace period before AI replies pause, with in-app and (where configured) notification warnings first. See the Help & Support page inside your dashboard for the exact timeline.

How do I pay?

Currently via GCash: upload a proof-of-payment screenshot from your Subscription page and a Developer approves it, usually same-day. A card/auto-charge option is on the roadmap — see the Launch Guide.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No. Cancel anytime from your Subscription page; there are no multi-month commitments or cancellation fees.
